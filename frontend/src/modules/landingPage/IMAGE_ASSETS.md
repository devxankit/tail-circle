## 4. Visual Consistency Guidelines

1. **Photographic Realism**: All photos must represent authentic pets with natural fur texture, true anatomical proportions, and realistic eye reflections. No caricatures or 3D animations.
2. **Color Harmony**: Backgrounds must complement the Tail Circle palette: Warm Yellow (`#FFC928`), Mint (`#DDF4EC`), Aqua (`#BFEFF0`), Soft Blue (`#DDEEFF`), Soft Pink (`#FCE5EC`), and Cream (`#FFF8EA`).
3. **Lighting**: Soft, diffused natural studio lighting and warm golden hour outdoor lighting.
